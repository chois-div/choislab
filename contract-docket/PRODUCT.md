## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js full-stack, as selected by the user.

## Users
Contract administrators at businesses who prepare agreements and send them to counterparties; counterparties who receive and sign agreements.

## Product Purpose
Create, review, send, sign, and receive business agreements electronically. Success means both the sender and recipient can complete and track the requested agreement workflow.

## Positioning
The user referenced Modusign as a comparable electronic-contract service. No distinct product differentiation has been established.

## Operating Context
The sender prepares an agreement, adds a direct signature or signature image, previews the document, sends a signing link by email, and receives the signed response.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The requested workflow includes drafting or editing agreements from CMS content or uploaded files such as PDFs; direct or image-based signing; preview; real email delivery by link; and returned agreements. Email delivery requires provider configuration outside source control. No email provider, identity verification method, production hosting, or jurisdiction-specific legal requirements have been specified.

## Brand Commitments
Modusign is a product reference only. Do not copy its logo, proprietary copy, or visual assets.

## Evidence on Hand
The user supplied the Modusign public website as a product reference. No customer data, legal templates, pricing, or proof claims have been supplied.

## Product Principles
- Make each step from drafting to returned agreement easy to find.
- Show a faithful preview before a sender requests signatures.
- Make the signer experience clear and usable from an email link.
- Do not imply legal validity, identity assurance, or delivery success without the corresponding configured capability.
